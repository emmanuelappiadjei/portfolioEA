Overview

From no site to found on Google.

JAFVISUAL is a photographer who had the work but nowhere to send people. I built the brand and the site from nothing: a new logo, a portfolio that doubles as a booking page, and everything it takes to put a business on the internet — the domain, the email, the DNS and the search setup.

The site went live in mid-September 2026. Google picked it up within two days, and in its first month it sat at the top of the results for the searches it appeared in.

My role

Everything, start to launch.

Brand identity, site design, build, and launch for a first-ever website. I designed the new logo, laid out the site in Figma, built it with Claude, bought the domain on GoDaddy, and handled the email, DNS and SEO setup to get it live and searchable.

Execution

Brand, build, launch, found.

01

New identity

A new logo and visual direction so the site, social, and booking all read as one brand.

02

Portfolio + booking

Designed in Figma and built with Claude: a portfolio that shows the work first and gives visitors a clear way to book a shoot.

03

Domain, email, DNS

Bought and configured the domain on GoDaddy, set up branded email, and wired up the DNS records to take the site live.

04

Search setup

SEO basics and Google Search Console from launch day, so the site was indexed and measurable from the start.
