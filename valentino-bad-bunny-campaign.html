Overview

Born from the heat.

Santo Sol is a speculative genderless fragrance collaboration between Bad Bunny and Valentino — pairing Valentino’s luxury identity with Bad Bunny’s bold style and Puerto Rican pride.

The scent blends citrus, florals, sea salt, amber, and warm woods to evoke the island’s sunlight, coastline, and nightlife, positioning fragrance as an expression of identity and confidence rather than a gendered category.

Campaign line: “Born from the heat. Worn without rules.”

Creative direction

Heat, rhythm & identity.

Inspired by the warmth, colour, and energy of Puerto Rico — where refined luxury meets bold self-expression. More than a celebrity fragrance, it’s a personal expression of culture, memory, and confidence.

Composition

Top, heart & base.

TopCitrus & bergamot — the island’s sunlight and tropical freshness.
HeartRose, hibiscus & sea salt — softness, confidence, and the air of the coast.
BaseAmber, musk, vanilla & warm woods — lingering heat, music-filled nights, the feeling of home.
