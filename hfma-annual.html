Overview

The campaign behind the deal.

Led the digital and email marketing push behind a major healthcare finance conference — building the campaign that drove awareness and turnout, then carrying that same marketing strategy through to a private suite and partner reception on-site.

The email campaign I designed and deployed became the throughline connecting digital outreach to an in-person moment that closed $1.5M in new business.

My role

Digital & email marketing.

Email campaign strategy, digital execution, and creative direction for a major healthcare finance conference activation — from the first outreach send through to the experience the campaign was inviting people into.

Execution

Inbox to in-person.

01

Designed, built, and formatted the campaign end to end — copy layout, visual structure, and responsive formatting across email clients.

02

Messaging strategy

Directed the campaign’s messaging, connecting digital outreach directly to a $1.5M deal closed at the partner reception it promoted.

03

Creative direction

Extended the campaign’s creative direction into the private suite experience, keeping messaging consistent from inbox to in-person.

04

Built contact lists, analysed post-campaign feedback, and managed follow-up outreach, extending the campaign’s return past the event.
