Revecore Executive Dinner

An intimate dinner for healthcare leaders.

A private evening of conversation, exceptional cuisine, and curated wine service for senior revenue cycle executives.

Hosted by Revecore at The Charles in Dallas, this invitation-only dinner is designed as a refined setting for meaningful peer discussion away from the conference floor. Space is intentionally limited to keep the evening personal, focused, and highly conversational.
