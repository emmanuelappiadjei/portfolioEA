Overview

Own it. Carry it. Create it.

A speculative Fender x Ibanez collaboration — a limited-edition instrument collection built around three artists reshaping guitar and bass culture: Thundercat, Steve Lacy, and Toro y Moi.

Campaign idea: Own the instrument. Carry the legacy. Create what comes next.

Strategy

A new generation of guitar icons.

Build a new generation of guitar icons — transforming instruments associated with Thundercat, Steve Lacy, and Toro y Moi into one collectible, limited-edition line, positioning Fender and Ibanez at the intersection of music, fashion, and creativity.

Audience

Who it’s for.

01

Primary audience

Music-driven consumers ages 18–34 who see instruments as creative tools and cultural objects — active across TikTok, Instagram, and Spotify.

02

Secondary audience

Established musicians, collectors, and longtime Fender or Ibanez customers seeking a rare artist-edition instrument.

Positioning

Heritage meets innovation.

03

Why Fender x Ibanez

Fender’s heritage and timeless design paired with Ibanez’s technical innovation — credible to experienced musicians, exciting to a younger audience.

04

The meeting point

A meeting point between the instruments that shaped music history and the artists shaping its future.
